The hourly rate stated above includes and satisfies the 25% casual loading required under the SCHADS Award. This loading is paid in lieu of entitlements such as paid annual leave, paid personal/carer's leave, and notice of termination that apply to permanent employees. All rates of pay set out in this agreement meet or exceed the minimum rates prescribed under the Social, Community, Home Care and Disability Services Industry Award 2010 for the applicable classification level.

Hours & Shift Arrangements
Casual Employment: Shifts are offered based on business needs. You are not guaranteed minimum hours. You may accept or decline shifts offered without penalty. Once you accept a shift, you are committed to working it.

Shift Cancellations: At least 24 hours notice required. Emergency cancellations (serious illness, emergency) must be reported immediately.

Timekeeping: All hours must be recorded in ConnectTeam. Wages paid fortnightly via bank transfer. Pay slips provided electronically.

Code of Conduct, Policies & Confidentiality Agreement

Binding Agreement — All Staff Members

NDIS Code of Conduct
Mandatory Requirement: All NDIS workers must comply with the NDIS Code of Conduct. Breaches may result in termination and reporting to the NDIS Commission.

As an NDIS Support Worker, I must:

  • Act with respect for individual rights — dignity, autonomy, privacy, and choice
  • Provide supports safely and competently — with care and skill
  • Act with integrity, honesty, and transparency
  • Promptly raise and act on concerns about quality or safety
  • Take all reasonable steps to prevent and respond to violence, exploitation, neglect, and abuse
  • Not engage in sexual misconduct with people with disability
Professional Conduct
Safeguarding & Mandatory Reporting
Zero Tolerance: Wander Community Care has zero tolerance for abuse, neglect, exploitation, or violence against people with disability.
Confidentiality & Privacy
Critical: Breaching confidentiality may result in immediate termination, legal action, and reporting to the NDIS Commission.
Social Media & Technology
NEVER: Post about participants, take photos/videos, or add participants on personal social media.
Continuing Obligations
Important: Confidentiality and privacy obligations continue even after employment ends.
